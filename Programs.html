Our Core Programs

Clothing the Needy

We collect and distribute gently used clothing, shoes, and accessories to individuals and families who cannot afford basic necessities.

Empowering Education

We provide school uniforms, stationery, school shoes, and literacy programs to help children break the cycle of poverty.

Cooking Meals

We prepare and deliver nutritious meals to children and families facing food insecurity.

Homework Help & Essentials

We support children with homework, playtime, provide cleaning essentials, and sanitary pads to girls in school.
