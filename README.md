## Website Purpose
WEBSITE PROPOSAL
1. Organisation Overview
Organisation Name:
Fanang Diatla Home Care

Brief History:
Fanang Diatla Home Care is a registered Non-Profit Organisation (NPO 307 945) based in the Moretele Local Municipality, North West Province (Maubane and Thulwe). Driven by the motto "Activated Faith," the organisation consists of 11 dedicated members committed to providing essential support to vulnerable children and families. Founded to restore dignity and empower the community, the organisation focuses on distributing clothing, providing educational support (uniforms and stationery), serving nutritious meals, and supplying essential items like sanitary pads.

Mission Statement:
To provide essential support, education, and hope to vulnerable children and families in our community, rooted in faith and driven by compassion.

Vision Statement:
To empower communities, restore dignity, and break the cycle of poverty by ensuring every child has access to basic needs, education, and a supportive environment.

Target Audience:
The website will target:

Donors: Individuals and businesses looking to donate funds, clothes, or food.

Volunteers: Community members wanting to offer their time and skills.

Beneficiaries: Families and schools in Moretele requiring assistance (who need to know how to reach out).

Partners: NGOs, government departments, and corporate sponsors looking for community outreach opportunities.

##2. Website Goals and Objectives
The purpose of the website is to establish a credible online presence, increase donations/volunteer sign-ups, and showcase the tangible impact the NGO is making in the community.

Website Goals:

Increase online visibility and trust (showcasing NPO status).

Generate more volunteer sign-ups and donation enquiries.

Showcase the organisation's impact through an image gallery and statistics.

Provide a seamless way for the community to contact the organisation or submit enquiries.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

Goal	KPI
Increase website traffic	500 website visitors per month
Generate volunteer leads	At least 20 volunteer form submissions per month
Increase donor engagement	10+ new donation or partnership enquiries per month
Showcase impact	Update gallery with new photos monthly
Improve user experience	Ensure 100% mobile responsiveness and fast load times

#3. Current Website Analysis
Fanang Diatla Home Care currently relies on word-of-mouth, WhatsApp, and direct community engagement.
Strengths:
Strong, personal relationships with the local community.
Active and dedicated team (11 members).
Clear, faith-driven mission.
Weaknesses:
No central online platform for donors to verify the NPO's legitimacy.
Difficult for external sponsors to find or contact the organisation.
Impact (photos, stats) is not publicly documented in a professional format.
Areas for Improvement:
The website should:
Clearly display the NPO registration number and contact details.
Showcase a gallery of past distributions and events.
Provide a clear "Get Involved" section for donations and volunteering.
Offer an easy-to-use contact form and embedded enquiry form.

#10.SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMISTATION
## 1. Define SEO and explain its purpose in web development.

Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) is the process of improving a website so that search engines can understand its content and display it in relevant search results. Its purpose is to increase website visibility, attract visitors and help customers find River Bite Restaurant online.

## 2. Explain how SEO practices can improve user experience (UX) and website performance.

SEO improves user experience by making website content easy to find, read and navigate. Responsive CSS allows the website to adapt to different screen sizes, while optimised images and well-structured HTML can improve loading and accessibility. Clear navigation helps customers find the menu, contact details and restaurant services.

## 3. Discuss the benefits of implementing SEO on a website.

SEO can increase website visibility, attract more potential customers and build trust in the restaurant. It can help people searching for restaurant menus and food services discover River Bite Restaurant. Good SEO can also improve website usability and support long-term business growth.

## 4. Explain and demonstrate specific SEO techniques using HTML, CSS and JavaScript where applicable.

HTML supports SEO through descriptive page titles, headings, meta descriptions, meaningful links and image alt text. CSS improves readability, mobile responsiveness and navigation, helping visitors use the website more easily. JavaScript adds functionality such as online ordering, automatic price calculations, form validation and checkout feedback, improving customer interaction.

## 5. Explain the purpose of HTML meta elements and demonstrate relevant examples.

HTML meta elements provide information about a webpage to browsers and search engines. The description meta tag summarises the page, the viewport tag supports mobile responsiveness, and the charset tag specifies character encoding. For example: `<meta name="description" content="Explore River Bite Restaurant's menu and food services.">`
